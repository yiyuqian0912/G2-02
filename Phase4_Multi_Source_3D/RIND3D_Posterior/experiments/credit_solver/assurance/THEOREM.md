# Release contract (candidate 2)

For every successfully issued artifact from the designated fixed CPU executable, for an authenticated input container in the declared population, let F be the measurable set of legal sources reproducing every FREE receiver, and q its normalized Lebesgue measure on the 128³ grid. CERTIFIED implies volume(F)>0 and TV(decode(wire),q) ≤ reported rational bound ≤ 1/20. No totality or runtime guarantee is claimed. A zero-measure feasible set has no such probability target and cannot earn positive lower credit.

The declared world has one canonical binary64, strictly world-contained, full-dimensional polyhedral union, 1–64 components, with at most 64 defining halfspaces each. Positive rational row rescalings preserve signs. The supported window has integer origin, side 64, 128 or 256, world resolution 1024 and a source-separated direct active/inactive FREE edge. SOLID at voxel centers is validated against the closed bodies. Sources occupy the open world outside the closed observation window, at Euclidean distance strictly greater than 2^-26 from each body. Segment contact blocks. The finite observations are inputs, irrespective of which generating source produced them. Null boundary ownership is immaterial to volume. Scoring an observation with a fitted posterior or training in a lower precision is outside the issued statement.

The theorem concerns honest executions of the frozen implementation and artifacts delivered under an independently trusted issuance digest. Unkeyed self-hashes alone do not authenticate authorship. It assumes private unmodified process state, valid canonical input origin, faithful binary64 decoding, specified Python/NumPy/Numba/LLVM integer and rounding semantics, operating system isolation and reliable storage. A heuristic can fail or the budget can expire while a valid earlier certificate exists. A failed scientific guard taints the computation and forbids release. There is no statistical fallback and no full replay assumption.
