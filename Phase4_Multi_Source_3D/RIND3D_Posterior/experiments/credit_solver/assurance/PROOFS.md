# From a finite response channel to issued posterior bytes

Read THEOREM.md for the claim on the supported entry point. This review concerns numerical commit acc2185. Arithmetic equalities below are mathematical results. The correspondence of Python and compiled code to those results is an implementation review, with differential tests and selected solver-checked implications. There is no whole-program verification.

## Single-facet integration and its applicability

Use the nonpositive sign convention for a supporting row (a,b,c,d). Each closed cell has x=(i+u)/128, y=(j+v)/128, z=(k+w)/128, with u,v,w in [0,1]. Distinct cell closures overlap only on measure-zero faces. Multiplying by 128 is exact. Reflect a coordinate t to 1−t when its coefficient is negative. A zero coefficient contributes a factor of one to volume. The resulting condition is Σ a'_i t_i ≤ h for n nonzero absolute coefficients a'_i, where h=−(128d+ai+bj+ck+min(a,0)+min(b,0)+min(c,0)). At least one coefficient is nonzero. For h≤0 the three-volume is zero, including tangency. For h≥Σa'_i it is the whole cell.

Substitute y_i=a'_i t_i. Without upper bounds the nonnegative simplex has n-volume h₊ⁿ/n! by n iterated integrations. To impose y_i≤a'_i, subtract the translated simplices with y_i>a'_i by inclusion–exclusion. Translation for subset J subtracts Σ_{j∈J}a'_j from h. The Jacobian contributes 1/Πa'_i. Coordinate reflections preserve measure, and strict versus non-strict upper bounds change no volume. This proves the implemented formula for n=1,2,3 including repeated widths and zero truncated powers. In the partial case its numerator lies strictly between zero and the positive denominator. Cancellation takes place in arbitrary-precision integers. The units are S=2^160 ticks per unit world volume, K=S/128³ ticks per cell. Integer divmod(K·num,den) yields floor and floor plus one iff the remainder is nonzero. Magnitude can cost time or memory but introduces no integer rounding beyond this directed conversion.

A structurally verified full-dimensional convex polytope is the intersection of its supporting facet halfspaces. facet_rows checks the rows against every vertex. For an inclusive integer block [i,I]×[j,J]×[k,K'], the continuous upper corner is (I+1,J+1,K'+1)/128. An affine form attains its two extrema at sign-selected corners. A row with minimum ≥0 excludes the block interior (a nonconstant zero set is null). Maximum ≤0 means it is satisfied throughout. Each other row remains active. Removing a satisfied row for descendants is sound by set inclusion. **Only zero or one remaining active facet permits the shortcut.** Duplicate relevant planes can cause extra splitting, never the loss of another cut. Two or more active facets trigger an exact split on an integer grid boundary, or, at unit-cell extent, exact rational volume of the already clipped polytope. Inductively the geometric object on the stack is the original polytope intersected with that block; the root's grid bounds contain its full positive volume. The leaves partition the volume modulo grid faces. The sum of floor credits cannot exceed the exact piece volume. Its gap is strictly less than the number of potentially rounded boundary contributions if positive, zero if the count is zero. The run-time conservation guard is a corroborating scalar identity. It could not on its own prove correct cell addresses.

Independent check: exact_reference.py enumerates intersections of *input* planes, tests all inequalities, orders each planar facet in a Cartesian projection and integrates x·n/3 on its surface. It does not call the solver's split, fan volume or single-facet formula. test_release_proofs.py compares floor and ceiling separately for reflected axes, near-corner thresholds with 257-bit multipliers, inactive axes and simultaneous cuts on each cell of a small grid. For a plane a·x+d=0 the projected area conversion gives flux −d/|a_axis| times projected area, independent of interior location. Singular triples are ignored. Lower-dimensional bounded intersections have zero volume. This reference is for bounded H-polytopes only.

## State and credit induction

Initially one cube is live, all FREE receivers are pending, and no volume has been credited. Conceptually the domain partitions into discarded, settled and live interiors. The stored maps aggregate settled pieces; a monotonically increasing handle names each live piece for one state only. Every feasible source belongs to settled or live space almost everywhere. Every settled source satisfies clearance and all observed labels almost everywhere.

* A geometric split keeps the disjoint half interiors. In an inverse-cone union, the first blocker in component order owns the point, with earlier blockers subtracted from subsequent parts. Active observations subtract every blocking cone. Excluding a window, a closed solid or a proven contradiction cannot remove a feasible open subset. A nonconstant source split can discard its boundary for integration, even when the original closed-segment physical contact test must include equality.
* A child inherits established receiver obligations by containment. For each unresolved receiver it retains all potentially blocking bodies. On buffer exhaustion the old obligation survives. Screen and exact enforcement cannot mark an arbitrary receiver complete on a search hint. A complete mixed-patch cover is required before its optional collective settlement. A partial cover retains the previous pending obligations.
* Settlement partitions legal interior, proven forbidden clearance, and deferred shell. The shell remains live. Each settled and validated full-dimensional piece contributes only its own cell-wise floor and ceiling. Multiple credits for one cell are added across disjoint pieces. All counts and sums in the ledger are Python integers.
* _commit constructs a replacement state before assigning it. Removed handles must all exist once. New identities cannot alias old ones. Live, lower and upper maps that change are copied before update. The lower total increments by the sum of the new lower delta. If the optional live total exists, subtract the exact ceilings of the removed pieces and add those of the children. Its initial value is a full sum. This preserves both cached identities by induction, including unchanged maps. A budget exception before assignment leaves the earlier partition; after assignment the new partition is already self-consistent. Aliasing assumptions cover same-process search and immutable scientific objects, not arbitrary Python reflection.
* Before making a snapshot eligible the kernel checks live topology, rescans all live ceilings, and checks the cached lower total against its authoritative map. Budget cancellation cannot partly publish a new map. A tainted kernel cannot issue even a former best result. Process death cannot publish an unvalidated worker attempt as a certificate. Resource failure may leave an older valid committed receipt. Final publication trusts the single owner and the persistence semantics documented in OPERATIONS.md.

## A necessary condition for success at a checkpoint

Let l_i and h_i be sums of downward/upward settled ticks, L=Σl_i, H=Σh_i. Let U=Σ ceil(S·vol(piece)) over current live pieces. For cell i, u_i counts only charges of live pieces contained wholly in i. These pieces are disjoint in volume. Their exact volumes total at most one cell, and each ceiling adds less than one tick. Hence u_i≤K+n for n live pieces, including n=0. A piece crossing multiple cells contributes its *entire* charge to U and to no u_i. Multiple live pieces in the same cell cause no undercharge. H−h_i≥L−l_i. Consequently

    l_i + (H−h_i) + (U−u_i) ≥ L + max(0,U−K−n) = L+e.

For L>0, the sum of the lower overlaps used by this implementation is at most L/(L+e). Taking a min with encoded probabilities and rounding down can only shrink that sum. The computed TV bound is therefore at least e/(L+e). If e·den(τ)>num(τ)·(L+e), this snapshot cannot issue. Equality is allowed to proceed. With L=0 the kernel cannot issue in the first place. The implementation consults this gate only for support ≥4096 and uses integer arithmetic throughout. Small positive mass does not introduce underflow. The test permits a snapshot; it never authorizes release. Release always checks its final state.

At a *fixed* state the bytes and bound are independent of whether the hint was evaluated. Skipping a snapshot under this inequality cannot skip a passing certificate along the same sequence of states. A guarantee of identical numerical final bounds between timed runs would be false: an omitted unsuccessful snapshot could have improved an INCOMPLETE diagnostic, time saved can change the trajectory, and the existing additional float progress heuristic may schedule checkpoints differently. The deterministic implication concerns every actually released label, independent of scheduling.

## The bound on the delivered distribution

Write v_i for the actual feasible volume in cell i in ticks. By ownership, v_i≥l_i, and feasible volume outside i is bounded by b_i=H−h_i+U−u_i. In particular this subtraction removes *only* definitely same-cell live charges. With positive settled lower mass, q_i≥l_i/(l_i+b_i) for l_i>0 by monotonicity in inside/outside mass. A zero lower credit gives lower bound zero, including sparse omissions. For normalized decoded p, TV(p,q)=1−Σmin(p_i,q_i). dyadic_bound sums floor-rounded lower overlaps in COMMON units and subtracts from one, hence rounds TV outward. COMMON is a multiple of the 2^52 probability tick denominator. Integer quotas, remainders and deterministic tie-breaking assign exactly 2^52 ticks over the positive lower support. The quotient k/2^52 is exactly representable in binary64. The validator consumes the entire little-endian stream, checks increasing unique in-range addresses, dyadic ticks, positivity, complete records and exact normalization. No dense-grid/support-size assumption enters the proof. The verifier compares the actual serialized bytes and scientific identity to the statement. For distribution outside the issuer trust boundary, read_issued also needs an independently trusted artifact digest and expected build.

training_conversion.audit separately computes the exact TV displacement after binary32 rounding followed by *exact* renormalization and adds it by the triangle inequality. Ordinary float32 summation/softmax/renormalization in a training framework is a different transform. Even when float32 sums exactly to one a change can consume the last fraction of the 0.05 allowance.
